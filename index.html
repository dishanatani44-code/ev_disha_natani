05 / Interpretation

Key takeaways

What the analysis supports

The Tableau views show variation across vehicle categories and model years, a battery-market composition dominated by the larger visible NMC/LFP slices, and a multi-region manufacturing distribution. The dashboard makes these dimensions available together.

What needs source verification

Sales-unit aggregation logic, model-count distinctness, manufacturers KPI, category-level rating interpretation and inconsistent region/country labels should be validated in Tableau before presenting the figures as authoritative EV market statistics.
